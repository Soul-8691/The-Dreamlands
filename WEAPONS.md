## How to read an entry

`- [ONE-03] **Name** — what it is · class · damage · scaling · Req · Wt · status · special. Prompt: "…" (where found)`

- **Class** decides the moveset (CMB-23: longsword, axe, mace, spear, greatsword). A few entries use a **new class** (dagger, halberd, great club, censer). That's marked, because each new class needs its own set of attack animations and frame data.
- **Damage** is the base damage and its type (CMB-18: slash, strike, pierce, fire, frost, radiant). It plugs into `raw = base × motion × scaling` (ROADMAP §7.3). For scale, a Bandit Brigand has 25 defense and 220 HP.
- **Scaling** uses the S/A/B/C/D/E grades for Might, Dexterity and Faith (STA-09). "—" means none.
- **Req** is the attribute requirement (all attributes start at 10). WPR-03 decides what happens below it.
- **Status** is buildup per hit (CMB-19: bleed, poison, burn, frost, stun).
- **Magic weapons** list a **Rite power** (how strong Rites cast through them are), a **cooldown** modifier for Rites (CMB-34), and sometimes a Rite they grant. Granted Rites are new spells, so approving the weapon approves its Rite too.
- **Prompt** is the Meshy prompt. The batch script adds the shared style line (ART-02).
- ⭐ = needed for the First Playable.

## Rules (WPR)

- [WPR-01] **Main hand, off hand, sidearm** — The main hand holds a weapon. The off hand holds **either** a shield **or** a magic weapon. Two-handed weapons and staves fill both hands. The hand crossbow (CMB-20) is a separate quick-draw sidearm, so it's always available. Rites need a magic weapon in the off hand, or the Rune Ring (MAG-16), which lets a sword-and-shield build cast at reduced power. ⭐ *(DS, SK)*
- [WPR-02] **Two-handing** — Two-handed weapons deal more poise damage (+30%), and their heavy attacks can't be interrupted. You give up the shield, so blocking falls back to a weak weapon guard. *(DS)*
- [WPR-03] **Under-requirement penalty** — You can always use a weapon. Below its requirement, it loses all scaling bonuses and every attack costs 25% more stamina. Magic weapons below their requirement cast with half Rite power. This matches the armor rule (ARS-01). ⭐ *(DS)*
- [WPR-04] **Smith upgrades** — Weapons and shields go from +1 to +10 at the smith, using ore (CMB-24, per your note). Each level adds +6% base damage (or stability for shields, or Rite power for magic weapons), and the scaling grades rise at +5 and +10. The Dawnblade is the exception: it only grows with Crown Shards (HER-10). ⭐ *(DS)*
- [WPR-05] **Rune socket** — Every non-starter weapon has one rune socket (CMB-25) for added elemental damage or an on-hit effect. *(SK, MW)*
- [WPR-06] **Meshy pipeline for weapons** — Each weapon is generated on its own, lying flat, with no hand. `normalize.py` puts the pivot at the grip, aligns the blade along one axis, attaches it to Rowan's hand bone, and builds the hitbox capsule from the blade's length (CMB-17). Shields attach to the forearm. *(DOC)*
- [WPR-07] **Weapon stat sheet** — Each weapon lists base damage per type, scaling grades (STA-09), requirements, weight, stamina per swing, poise damage and crit multiplier, all in one JSON entry. *(DS)*
- [WPR-08] **Split damage** — A weapon can deal two types at once (the Dawnblade: slash plus radiant), and each part is reduced by the target's own defence for that type. *(DS)*
- [WPR-09] **Shield stats** — Stability (stamina lost per blocked hit), physical block %, and elemental block % for fire, frost and radiant. *(DS)*
- [WPR-10] **Rite power** — Magic weapons have a Rite power value (100 at base) that rises with Faith and upgrades. A Rite's effect is its base value × Rite power / 100. *(DS)*
- [WPR-11] **Rite stat sheet** — Each Rite lists its base power, damage type, range, cooldown (CMB-34) and Faith requirement, in the same JSON style as weapons. *(DS, NEW)*
